Fesenjan

Walnut and pomegranate molasses: earthy, sour, and slightly sweet.

20 min prep · 1 hr 20 min cook · Serves 6 · about 5 cups

History

Khoresh-e fesenjan is the Persian stew of walnuts and pomegranate, classically with duck or chicken, served with rice. It is associated with the Caspian provinces, especially Gilan. The sauce is the point: walnuts are ground and cooked until the sauce turns deep and the oil separates a little. It is a braise, eaten as a sauce over rice.

Cultural background

Celebration tables in Iran include it. The balance of sour pomegranate and rich walnut is the skill. Sugar is used only if the molasses is very sharp.

Why it tastes this way

Ground walnuts cooked a long time taste round, and pomegranate molasses makes the sauce sour. The dark color is the long cook, not extra spice. Duck or chicken takes on that sauce.

How it is made

Ground walnuts simmer in stock until creamy, pomegranate molasses is added, and chicken or duck cooks in the sauce.

Authentic touch

Stir the walnuts often and cook them until they no longer taste raw before you judge the sweet and sour.

How it is used

Serve it over chelo, Persian rice, with the poultry that simmered in it.

Field notes

  • The walnuts have to cook long enough to lose their raw flavor.
  • Fesenjan is a stew sauce. The rice is the plate, not a side thought.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups walnuts, finely ground
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1/2 cup pomegranate molasses
  • 1 tablespoon sugar, optional
  • 1 pound chicken thighs
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Brown the chicken in the oil and set it aside.
  2. Stir the walnuts and stock and simmer 30 minutes, stirring often.
  3. Add the molasses and sugar if you want it rounder.
  4. Return the chicken and simmer until cooked through and the sauce is dark and thick, about 40 minutes.
  5. Salt and serve with rice.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A meatless version uses the same sauce over roasted eggplant and needs vegetable stock.
  • More molasses makes it sharper. A spoon of sugar rounds a harsh batch.

Pairings

  • rice
  • chicken
  • duck
