Dijon Mustard

Brown mustard seed and white wine or verjuice: sharp, clean, and hot.

10 min prep · 8 min cook · Serves 16 · about 1 cup

History

Dijon has been a mustard city since the Middle Ages. The pale, sharp style associated with it was shaped in the nineteenth century, when Jean Naigeon replaced vinegar with verjuice, the juice of unripe grapes. Modern Dijon mustard is a protected style in France, made from brown or black mustard seeds and a wine acid. This is a home mustard in that style, not a certified product.

Cultural background

It is a table mustard and a cooking ingredient for vinaigrettes, lapin à la moutarde, and cream sauces. It is hotter and less sweet than American yellow mustard.

Why it tastes this way

Brown mustard seed and wine or verjuice taste sharp and clean. Vinegar-only mustard tastes harsher. The heat fades if you boil it hard, which is why the simmer is short.

How it is made

Ground mustard seed soaks with wine and salt, then is simmered briefly and rested so the bitterness settles.

Authentic touch

Simmer briefly and rest it a day. Judge the heat tomorrow, not the minute you mix it.

How it is used

Use it on meat, in vinaigrette, or as the heat in a sauce.

Field notes

  • Verjuice, not vinegar, is what pushed Dijon’s mustard toward its winey sharpness.
  • The heat fades if the mustard is boiled hard. A short simmer is enough.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup brown mustard seeds, ground
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Pinch of sugar

Steps

  1. Stir the ground seed, wine, vinegar, salt, and sugar.
  2. Rest 10 minutes.
  3. Simmer 3 minutes over low heat, stirring.
  4. Cool. It will thicken.
  5. Refrigerate a day before judging the heat.

Refrigerate. The flavor settles over a day and keeps for weeks. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • American yellow mustard is milder and colored with turmeric.
  • Whole-grain mustard leaves the seeds partly crushed.

Pairings

  • roast pork
  • vinaigrette
  • ham
