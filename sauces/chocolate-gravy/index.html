Chocolate Gravy

Cocoa, milk, and sugar: bittersweet, thick, and breakfast-soft.

5 min prep · 12 min cook · Serves 4 · about 2 cups

History

Chocolate gravy is a breakfast sauce of the Appalachian mountains and parts of the upland South, especially Kentucky, Tennessee, West Virginia, and Arkansas. Cocoa, flour, milk, and sugar are cooked into a pourable gravy for biscuits. It is home cooking, not a restaurant dessert sauce, and it is not a Cherokee recipe.

Cultural background

Mountain tables served it when cocoa was on hand and oven sweets were not the point. Families still argue about how sweet it should be. The biscuit is the plate.

Why it tastes this way

Cocoa is bitter. Sugar and milk make it sweet enough for breakfast, and a flour roux makes it a gravy rather than hot chocolate. Butter carries the cocoa fat. That is why it tastes like a sauce you pour, not a cup you drink.

How it is made

Butter, flour, and cocoa make a roux, then milk and sugar simmer into a gravy.

Authentic touch

Cook the cocoa in the butter and flour for a minute so it does not taste dusty. Keep it pourable. It should soak a split biscuit.

How it is used

Pour it over hot biscuits at breakfast.

Field notes

  • It is a breakfast gravy, served where other tables would pour sausage gravy.
  • It is Appalachian home cooking, not a Native American ceremonial dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

Steps

  1. Melt the butter and stir in the flour and cocoa. Cook 1 minute.
  2. Whisk in the milk, sugar, and salt.
  3. Simmer until it coats a spoon, about 6 minutes.
  4. Stir in the vanilla.
  5. Serve over biscuits.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A spoon of vanilla is common.
  • Less sugar leaves it more bitter, which some tables prefer.

Pairings

  • biscuits
  • breakfast
