Tikka Masala Sauce

Tomato, cream, and spices: mildly hot, sweet, and rich.

15 min prep · 35 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 cups

History

Chicken tikka masala is a British restaurant dish: grilled spiced chicken in a tomato and cream sauce. Several UK origin stories circulate, including a Glasgow claim, and they do not agree. This page does not name an inventor. The sauce is a later, creamier cousin of North Indian makhani, built for the British curry house.

Cultural background

It is the defining British curry-house gravy, milder and often sweeter than restaurant curries in India. The chicken is cooked separately and then simmered in the sauce.

Why it tastes this way

A British restaurant sauce of tomato, spice, and cream tastes milder and often sweeter than many curries in India. The chicken is grilled first, so the sauce tastes of spice and tomato, not of boiled meat.

How it is made

Onion, tomato, and spices simmer, the sauce is blended, and cream finishes it.

Authentic touch

Grill or sear the chicken before it simmers in the sauce. Do not claim a single inventor. The stories disagree.

How it is used

Simmer grilled chicken tikka in the sauce and serve with rice or naan.

Field notes

  • The dish is British restaurant cooking. The origin stories disagree, so none is treated as fact.
  • The chicken is usually grilled first, then sauced.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 1 tablespoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 2 cups tomato puree
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Steps

  1. Cook the onion, garlic, and ginger in the oil.
  2. Add the spices and tomato and simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Blend smooth.
  4. Stir in the cream, salt, and sugar.
  5. Simmer grilled chicken in the sauce 10 minutes.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Makhani is the Delhi butter sauce and is close, not identical.
  • A spicier house version raises the chili.

Pairings

  • grilled chicken
  • rice
  • naan
