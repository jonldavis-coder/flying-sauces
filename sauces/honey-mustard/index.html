Mustard and honey: sharp and sweet.

5 min prep · 0 min cook · Serves 8 · about 1 cup

History

Honey mustard is a simple American dip and sandwich sauce of mustard and honey, sometimes with mayonnaise. Mustard preserves and honey are much older. The mild, sweet dip for chicken tenders became a standard in the late twentieth century. This is the two-ingredient balance.

Cultural background

It is a dipping sauce for salty fried food. Equal parts make it both sweet and sharp. Mayonnaise makes a creamier sandwich spread and adds egg.

Why it tastes this way

Mustard is sharp and hot. Honey is thick and sweet. Together they cling to salty food. The heat mellows as it sits, which is true of most prepared mustards.

How it is made

Mustard and honey are stirred together.

Authentic touch

Stir Dijon or yellow mustard with honey and wait 15 minutes. Taste and add more honey only if the mustard still shouts.

How it is used

Use it as a dip or a sandwich spread.

Field notes

  • The sauce is a balance. Too much honey and the mustard disappears.
  • A short rest mellows the heat.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Dijon mustard
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Stir everything together.
  2. Rest 15 minutes.
  3. Taste.
  4. Serve as a dip.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Mayonnaise makes it creamier.
  • Yellow mustard makes it milder than Dijon.

Pairings

  • chicken tenders
  • pretzels
  • ham
