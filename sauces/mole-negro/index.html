Mole Negro

The next field stop is a nearby sauce on the same map.

Next stop: Mole Poblano · Mexico

Charred chile, nut, and spice: dark, smoky, and complex.

40 min prep · 1 hr 20 min cook · Serves 8 · about 6 cups

History

Mole negro is the dark Oaxacan mole, built on chilhuacle chiles when they can be found, with other dried chiles, nuts, seeds, spices, and a controlled char that blackens some of the chile. It is not mole poblano. Chocolate may appear in a small amount. The darkness is toast and char, not cocoa alone.

Cultural background

It is feast food in Oaxaca, served with turkey or chicken. Chilhuacle negro is the regional chile. Ancho and mulato are stand-ins and will not taste identical. This recipe says so.

Why it tastes this way

The black taste is charred chile and toasted seed, fried as a puree. A little chocolate seasons. Too much chocolate makes it taste like dessert. The char has to stop before bitterness.

How it is made

Dried chiles, nuts, and seeds are toasted, blended with tomato and spice, and fried with stock.

Authentic touch

Toast some chile until very dark but not ashy, blend, then fry the puree in fat until it looks glossy and the raw taste is gone.

How it is used

Serve it with turkey or chicken and rice or tortillas.

Field notes

  • The color is char and toast, not a bottle of cocoa.
  • Chilhuacle chiles are the Oaxacan ideal and are often scarce.

Ingredients

  • 4 dried ancho chiles
  • 3 dried guajillo chiles
  • 2 dried mulato chiles
  • 2 tablespoons lard or oil
  • 1/4 cup almonds
  • 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 1 ounce Mexican chocolate
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Toast the chiles until fragrant and dark at the edges, soak them, and drain.
  2. Toast the nuts and seeds. Char the onion and tomato.
  3. Blend everything with 1 cup stock.
  4. Fry the puree in the fat 10 minutes.
  5. Add the rest of the stock and chocolate and simmer 40 minutes. Salt and serve.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Mole poblano is a different chile blend and is less black.
  • Without chilhuacle, the sauce is an approximation.

Pairings

  • turkey
  • chicken
  • rice
