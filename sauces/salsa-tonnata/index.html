Salsa Tonnata

Tuna, capers, anchovy, and mayonnaise: savory, briny, and creamy.

15 min prep · 0 min cook · Serves 6 · about 1 1/2 cups

History

Salsa tonnata is the Piedmontese tuna sauce for vitello tonnato, cold veal covered with a tuna and mayonnaise sauce. The combination of boiled meat and preserved tuna is a Piedmontese classic, written into Italian cooking well before it became an international summer dish. Capers and anchovy are part of the sauce.

Cultural background

The veal is boiled or roasted, cooled, and sliced. The sauce is made while the meat cools. Leftovers are the point of the dish as much as the first plating.

Why it tastes this way

Tuna, capers, and anchovy blended into mayonnaise taste savory and briny. The meat underneath is mild and cold, so the sauce carries the flavor. It is a cold sauce because the dish is served cold.

How it is made

Tuna, capers, anchovy, and lemon are blended into mayonnaise until smooth.

Authentic touch

Blend the tuna and anchovy smooth before you fold in the mayonnaise. A chunky sauce will not coat sliced meat.

How it is used

Spoon it over cold sliced veal, pork, or boiled potatoes.

Field notes

  • The tuna is in the sauce, not beside the meat as a steak.
  • Vitello tonnato is served cold, which is why the sauce is a mayonnaise.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (5 ounces) tuna in oil, drained
  • 2 anchovy fillets
  • 1 tablespoon capers
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon of the tuna oil or olive oil

Steps

  1. Blend the tuna, anchovy, capers, lemon, and oil until smooth.
  2. Stir in the mayonnaise.
  3. Loosen with a spoon of water if it is pasty.
  4. Season only if it needs salt.
  5. Spoon over cold sliced meat.

The mayonnaise base should stay refrigerated.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A little of the meat’s cooking liquid loosens the sauce.
  • Chicken or turkey can stand in for veal. The sauce stays the same.

Pairings

  • cold veal
  • cold pork
  • potatoes
