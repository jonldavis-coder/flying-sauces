Toum

Garlic and oil, whipped: fluffy, sharp, and intense.

15 min prep · 0 min cook · Serves 8 · about 1 1/2 cups

History

Toum is the Lebanese garlic sauce served with grilled chicken, especially shawarma and rotisserie chicken. Garlic, oil, lemon, and salt are whipped into a white emulsion. Egg is not required. The garlic itself holds the oil if it is added slowly.

Cultural background

It is the sauce in a chicken shawarma wrap and on the plate with toum-painted grilled chicken. A little goes a long way.

Why it tastes this way

Raw garlic emulsified with oil tastes intensely of garlic because there is no cooking to soften it and often no egg to dilute it. Lemon keeps the emulsion bright and white.

How it is made

Garlic and salt are blended, then oil is added in a thin stream with lemon until the sauce turns fluffy and white.

Authentic touch

Add the oil in a thin stream. If it breaks, start with a fresh garlic clove and beat the broken sauce in slowly. Refrigerate it. Do not leave it at room temperature.

How it is used

Serve it with grilled chicken or as a sandwich spread.

Field notes

  • Toum can emulsify without egg. The garlic is the emulsifier.
  • It is meant to be very garlicky. A mild toum is under-seasoned.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peeled garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups neutral oil
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons ice water

Steps

  1. Blend the garlic and salt to a paste.
  2. With the motor running, add a few drops of oil until the paste looks sticky and white.
  3. Alternate small additions of oil and lemon, then the ice water.
  4. Stop when the sauce is fluffy. If it tastes harsh, rest it 10 minutes.
  5. Refrigerate.

Refrigerate and use within a few days. Do not store garlic-in-oil at room temperature.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A pasteurized egg white makes the emulsion easier and is no longer egg-free.
  • If it breaks, start again with a fresh garlic clove and beat the broken sauce in slowly.

Pairings

  • chicken shawarma
  • grilled chicken
  • french fries
