Recado Rojo

This stop is on Mexico to the Andes, traveled by plane.

Next stop: Ají (Ecuador) · Ecuador · Mexico to the Andes

Annatto, garlic, oregano, and sour orange: earthy, sharp, and savory.

15 min prep · 0 min cook · Serves 8 · about 1 cup

History

Recado rojo is the Yucatecan achiote paste used for cochinita pibil and other pit or oven meats. Annatto seeds dye it red and taste earthy. Sour orange, garlic, and oregano are the rest. It is not a tomato marinade.

Cultural background

Banana leaves wrap pibil meats and add a green aroma when they are used. The paste is the seasoning. Sour orange is the Yucatán acid, as in Cuban mojo, and lime plus orange is the stand-in.

Why it tastes this way

Annatto tastes earthy and colors the meat. Sour orange keeps it sharp. Oregano and garlic perfume the fat. Without the citrus, achiote paste tastes flat and dusty.

How it is made

Annatto, garlic, spices, and sour orange are blended into a paste that coats pork or chicken.

Authentic touch

Grind or blend the annatto with garlic and sour orange into a wet paste. Rub it on the meat and marinate. If you wrap in banana leaf, warm the leaf so it does not crack.

How it is used

Slow-cook the marinated meat until tender and serve it with tortillas and pickled onion.

Field notes

  • The red is annatto, not paprika alone.
  • Sour orange is the traditional acid of the Yucatán paste.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons annatto seeds, ground, or 1/4 cup achiote paste
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 cup sour orange juice or half lime and half orange
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons oil

Steps

  1. Blend all the ingredients into a wet paste.
  2. Rub it over pork shoulder.
  3. Refrigerate at least 4 hours.
  4. Cook covered until tender.
  5. Serve with tortillas.

Cook the meat through. Do not eat the raw paste.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Recado blanco and recado negro are different Yucatecan pastes.
  • Equal parts lime and orange approximate sour orange.

Pairings

  • pork
  • warm tortillas
  • pickled red onion
