Pebre

This stop is on Mexico to the Andes, traveled by plane.

Onion, tomato, cilantro, aji, and vinegar: fresh, sharp, and hot.

20 min prep · 0 min cook · Serves 6 · about 2 cups

History

Pebre is the Chilean table salsa of onion, tomato or sometimes just onion, cilantro, aji chile, oil, and vinegar or lemon. It is served with bread, grilled meat, and empanadas. It is not Argentine chimichurri, which is parsley-heavy and usually tomato-free.

Cultural background

A bowl of pebre and bread starts many Chilean meals. The heat depends on the aji. Recipes vary by household more than by a single official formula.

Why it tastes this way

Chile, onion, tomato, cilantro, and vinegar are chopped, not cooked, so pebre tastes fresh and hot. Rinsing the onion keeps its raw bite from covering the chile and tomato.

How it is made

Onion is rinsed or salted, then mixed with tomato, cilantro, chile, vinegar, and oil. It is not cooked.

Authentic touch

Rinse the diced onion and drain it well. Then salt the bowl and let it sit so the juices come out.

How it is used

Eat it with bread, choripán, or grilled meat.

Field notes

  • Pebre is a Chilean salsa, not a name for chimichurri.
  • Rinsing the onion keeps the raw bite from taking over the bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 small onion, finely diced and rinsed
  • 2 tomatoes, seeded and diced
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 fresh aji or serrano chile, minced
  • 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Rinse the diced onion under cold water and drain it well.
  2. Toss it with the tomato, cilantro, and chile.
  3. Add vinegar, oil, and salt.
  4. Rest 15 minutes.
  5. Serve with bread or grilled meat.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Some pebre skips tomato and is mostly onion and chile.
  • Chancho en piedra is a mashed Chilean tomato-chile sauce and a different recipe.

Pairings

  • marraqueta bread
  • choripán
  • grilled beef
