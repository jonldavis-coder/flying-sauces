Creole Sauce

This stop is on Across the American South, traveled by train.

Next stop: Louisiana-Style Hot Sauce · United States · Across the American South

Tomato, onion, celery, and bell pepper: savory, sweet, and lightly herbal.

15 min prep · 35 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 cups

History

Creole sauce is the tomato sauce of Louisiana built on onion, celery, and bell pepper, cooked with garlic and thyme and served with shrimp. The three vegetables are the local aromatic base. It is a tomato sauce, distinct from a brown roux étouffée.

Cultural background

Shrimp Creole is the dish, eaten with rice. The vegetables should cook until soft before the tomato goes in. Cayenne heats it. The sauce should taste of pepper and celery, not only tomato.

Why it tastes this way

Bell pepper, celery, and onion cooked in oil taste sweet and savory. Tomato and thyme make a round sauce. A short cook keeps it brighter than a long Sunday gravy.

How it is made

The aromatic vegetables soften, then tomato, garlic, and thyme simmer.

Authentic touch

Cook the onion, celery, and pepper until soft before you add tomato. That base is the flavor.

How it is used

Serve it with shrimp and rice.

Field notes

  • Onion, celery, and green pepper are the Louisiana aromatic trio in this sauce.
  • It is tomato-based, unlike a brown étouffée.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 can (28 ounces) tomatoes, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Cook the onion, pepper, and celery in the oil until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, tomatoes, thyme, cayenne, and salt.
  3. Simmer 25 minutes.
  4. Add cooked shrimp at the end if you are making the dish.
  5. Serve with rice.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A roux makes it closer to étouffée.
  • More cayenne is a household choice.

Pairings

  • shrimp
  • rice
