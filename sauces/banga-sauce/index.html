Banga Sauce

Palm fruit, crayfish, and chile: rich, earthy, and aromatic.

15 min prep · 40 min cook · Serves 6 · about 5 cups

History

Ofe banga is the palm-fruit soup of the Niger Delta, especially Urhobo and Itsekiri cooking. Palm fruit extract is the body. Scent leaves, crayfish, and local spices such as ataiko and oburunbebe are traditional when you can buy them. This recipe uses canned palm cream and names those spices as optional, because a random substitute would not be the same.

Cultural background

It is served with starch such as starch swallow or rice, often with dried or fresh fish. The flavor is palm, not tomato.

Why it tastes this way

Palm-fruit cream tastes rich and earthy, different from tomato. Chile and crayfish season it. Delta spices, when you can buy them, are an extra aroma this pot does not fake.

How it is made

Palm cream is simmered with onion, crayfish, chile, and stock until it becomes a thick sauce.

Authentic touch

Simmer gently so the palm cream does not split into grease. Use a prepared palm base.

How it is used

Serve it with fish and a starch.

Field notes

  • Banga is palm-fruit extract, which is why the sauce is naturally thick and reddish.
  • The Delta spice seeds are part of the aroma when you can get them, and this recipe does not fake them.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (14 ounces) palm cream or banga extract
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ground crayfish
  • 1 scotch bonnet, minced
  • 2 cups fish or meat stock
  • Salt
  • 1 pound fish steaks

Steps

  1. Simmer the palm cream, onion, crayfish, chile, and stock 15 minutes.
  2. Add the fish and salt.
  3. Simmer until the fish is cooked and the sauce is thick.
  4. Do not boil it so hard that the palm cream splits into grease.
  5. Serve with starch.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Ataiko and oburunbebe are the Delta spices. Use them if you can buy them. Do not invent a swap.
  • Congolese moambe is a related palm sauce with a different spice path.

Pairings

  • starch swallow
  • dried fish
  • rice
