Rouille

Garlic, chile, olive oil, and a little broth: rusty red, hot, and thick.

History

Rouille is the Provençal sauce served with bouillabaisse and fish soups. The name means rust, from the color of dried chile or saffron in the emulsion. Classic versions pound garlic, chile, and sometimes soaked bread or potato, then mount the paste with olive oil and a spoon of the fish broth.

Cultural background

It is spread on toast that goes into the soup, or stirred into the bowl. It is not the soup itself. Aïoli is the garlic emulsion without the rust-colored chile.

Why it tastes this way

Garlic, chile, and olive oil taste rusty and hot because dried chile colors and heats an emulsion meant for fish broth. A spoon of the broth ties the sauce to the soup instead of leaving it like plain mayonnaise.

How it is made

Garlic, chile, and a little bread or cooked potato are pounded, then olive oil is worked in and the paste is loosened with hot fish broth.

Authentic touch

Loosen the finished emulsion with a ladle of the hot fish soup. That broth is part of the flavor.

How it is used

Serve it with fish soup and grilled bread.

Field notes

  • The rust color is the chile or saffron, which is how the sauce got its name.
  • It is a cousin of aïoli, built to meet a fish broth.

Ingredients

  • 1 small cooked potato
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne or a soaked dried chile
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons hot fish stock
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Pound or mash the potato, garlic, and chile.
  2. Work in the yolk.
  3. Stream in the oil as for mayonnaise.
  4. Loosen with the hot stock.
  5. Salt and serve with fish soup.

If you use a raw yolk, refrigerate the sauce and use it the same day.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A saffron version is milder in chile and more floral.
  • Potato instead of bread changes the texture and removes the wheat if the potato is plain.

Pairings

  • bouillabaisse
  • fish soup
  • grilled bread
