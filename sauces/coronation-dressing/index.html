Coronation Chicken Dressing

The next field stop is a nearby sauce on the same map.

Next stop: Cumberland Sauce · United Kingdom

Mayonnaise, curry powder, and apricot or mango: creamy, spiced, and sweet.

10 min prep · 0 min cook · Serves 6 · about 1 1/2 cups

History

Coronation chicken was created for the 1953 coronation of Elizabeth II by Constance Spry and Rosemary Hume. The original sauce for the cold chicken included mayonnaise, curry, and a reduced apricot and tomato mixture. Later British versions simplified it into a curry-mayonnaise dressing with mango chutney. This is that simpler dressing, named for the occasion.

Cultural background

It became a sandwich-shop and buffet standard in Britain. It is a cold sauce for cold chicken, not a hot curry.

Why it tastes this way

Cold mayonnaise, curry powder, and chutney taste creamy, spiced, and sweet because the 1953 chicken dish was a cold buffet sauce, not a hot curry. The spice is bloomed so the powder does not taste raw.

How it is made

Mayonnaise is stirred with curry powder, chutney, and lemon.

Authentic touch

Stir the curry powder with a spoon of water before it meets the mayonnaise. Fold it through cold chicken, not hot.

How it is used

Fold it through cold chicken for sandwiches or a salad.

Field notes

  • The dish was made for the 1953 coronation banquet’s catering, not invented by a later supermarket.
  • Curry powder in a cold mayonnaise is the British reading of the idea.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon mango chutney, chopped
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Stir the curry powder with the water to make a paste.
  2. Mix it with the mayonnaise, chutney, and lemon.
  3. Salt to taste.
  4. Fold through cold cooked chicken.
  5. Serve cold.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • The 1953 sauce used a cooked apricot reduction and was more elaborate.
  • Yogurt lightens the mayonnaise.

Pairings

  • cold chicken
  • sandwiches
  • lettuce
