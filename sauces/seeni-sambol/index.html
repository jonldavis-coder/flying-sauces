Seeni Sambol

Caramelized onion, chile, and tamarind: sweet, hot, and dark.

10 min prep · 35 min cook · Serves 6 · about 1 1/2 cups

History

Seeni sambol is the Sri Lankan caramelized onion relish, cooked dark with chile, curry leaves, and often Maldive fish and tamarind. Seeni refers to sugar. It is a filling for hoppers and a side for rice. This version uses a little fish sauce in place of Maldive fish when those flakes are hard to find, and says so.

Cultural background

It is spooned into egg hoppers and beside kiribath, the milk rice of celebrations. The onion has to cook until it is truly brown.

Why it tastes this way

Onion cooked until dark tastes sweet. Chile, tamarind, and a little fish sauce keep it savory. The sugar in the name is real, and the long browning is why it is not a pale onion fry.

How it is made

Onions caramelize slowly with chile and curry leaves, then tamarind, sugar, and fish sauce finish the relish.

Authentic touch

Give the onion the full time to go deep brown. Pale onion is not seeni sambol.

How it is used

Use it in hoppers or next to rice.

Field notes

  • The onion is cooked until dark. A pale onion relish is not seeni sambol.
  • Sugar is in the name, and the chile keeps it from being only sweet.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 3 onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon chili flakes
  • 8 curry leaves
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon tamarind paste
  • 1 teaspoon fish sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Cook the onions in the oil over medium-low heat until deep brown, about 25 minutes.
  2. Add the chile, curry leaves, and sugar.
  3. Stir in the tamarind, fish sauce, and salt.
  4. Cook 5 minutes more until jammy.
  5. Cool and serve with hoppers or rice.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Skip the fish sauce for a different, fully plant relish.
  • Maldive fish is the more traditional savory note.

Pairings

  • egg hoppers
  • milk rice
  • rice and curry
