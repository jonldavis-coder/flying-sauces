Muhammara

Roasted red pepper, walnut, and pomegranate molasses: sweet, tangy, and rich.

15 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 8 · about 2 cups

History

Muhammara is the walnut and red-pepper dip of Aleppo. Roasted peppers, walnuts, pomegranate molasses, and breadcrumbs are pounded with olive oil and mild Aleppo pepper. It is a meze, not a salsa. Turkish acuka is a related walnut-pepper paste and is treated here as a cousin, not a second recipe.

Cultural background

It is eaten with bread at the start of a meal. The pomegranate molasses is the sour-sweet note that tomato alone does not give.

Why it tastes this way

Roasted pepper, walnut, and pomegranate molasses taste smoky, rich, and sweet-sour. The molasses, not plain sugar, is the Aleppo sour-sweet. Bread crumbs stretch the nuts.

How it is made

Roasted peppers and toasted walnuts are blended with pomegranate molasses, bread crumbs, and oil.

Authentic touch

Roast the peppers until the skins blacken and toast the walnuts. Raw pepper and raw nuts taste flat.

How it is used

Serve it with warm bread or next to grilled vegetables.

Field notes

  • Pomegranate molasses, not sugar, is the traditional sweet-sour.
  • Aleppo is the city most closely tied to the dip.

Ingredients

  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 cup walnuts
  • 2 tablespoons pomegranate molasses
  • 1/2 cup fresh bread crumbs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or mild chili flakes

Steps

  1. Roast the peppers until blackened, steam, peel, and seed them.
  2. Toast the walnuts until fragrant.
  3. Blend the peppers, walnuts, molasses, crumbs, oil, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. The dip should be thick and spreadable.
  5. Serve at room temperature.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Aleppo pepper is mild and fruity. Cayenne makes it harsher.
  • Skipping the breadcrumbs makes a thicker, gluten-free paste if the other ingredients are gluten-free.

Pairings

  • pita
  • grilled eggplant
  • raw vegetables
