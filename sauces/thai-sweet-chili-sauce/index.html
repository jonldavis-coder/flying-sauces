Thai Sweet Chili Sauce

Sugar, vinegar, garlic, and chile: sweet, sharp, and lightly hot.

5 min prep · 10 min cook · Serves 6 · about 1 cup

History

Nam chim kai is the Thai sweet chile dipping sauce served with grilled chicken. Sugar, vinegar, garlic, and chile are simmered and lightly thickened. The bottled sweet chile sauce known worldwide is this family. It is milder and much sweeter than nam pla prik.

Cultural background

Grilled chicken stalls give you a small bag of it. Cucumber relish is the other usual side. This sauce is the sweet one.

Why it tastes this way

Sugar and vinegar are the taste, with garlic and a little chile. It is sweet because it is the dip for grilled chicken, not the salty fish-sauce condiment.

How it is made

Sugar, vinegar, water, garlic, and chile simmer, then a little cornstarch glosses the sauce.

Authentic touch

Simmer the sugar and vinegar until they taste balanced, then gloss with a little starch. It should still be sour.

How it is used

Serve it with grilled chicken.

Field notes

  • The grilled-chicken sauce is supposed to be sweet. Salt is not the main note.
  • It is a different bowl from the fish-sauce chile condiment.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon chile flakes
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Simmer the sugar, water, vinegar, garlic, and chile 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the cornstarch mixed with a spoon of water.
  3. Cook until glossy.
  4. Add the salt.
  5. Cool and serve with grilled chicken.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • More chile makes it closer to a hot sauce and less like the chicken dip.
  • A blender makes the garlic and chile finer, which is the bottled texture.

Pairings

  • gai yang
  • spring rolls
  • cucumber relish
