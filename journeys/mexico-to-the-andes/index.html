Plane

Mexico to the Andes

A flight from mole and achiote to the chile sauces of the Andes.

Chile is the thread, and the plane is the honest way to cover this distance. Puebla’s mole, Yucatán’s achiote paste, then the raw and cooked chile sauces of the Andes.

Each stop uses a different chile and a different thickener: nuts and seeds, annatto and sour orange, then ají and herbs.

Stops

  1. Arriving in Puebla: Mole Poblano
  2. Arriving in Mexico: Salsa Verde
  3. Arriving in Yucatán: Recado Rojo
  4. Arriving in Ecuador: Ají (Ecuador)
  5. Arriving in Peru: Ají Amarillo Sauce
  6. Arriving in Arequipa: Ocopa Sauce
  7. Arriving in Chile: Pebre
